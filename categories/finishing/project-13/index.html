Coastal Design - Creating a new model of luxury beach apartment living, everything is considered, from the macro down to the micro and the same attention is given on all details. The materials palette is soft and natural. Drawing from the hospitality industry, the apartment combine the warmth and tradition of a hotel with the grand hotel’s extroversion and comfort, and the boutique hotel’s fashionable, informal charm. Their refined, coastal design is complemented by more traditional country elements, such as white washed oak finishes and wooden floors, and large windows accentuate the sea views.
